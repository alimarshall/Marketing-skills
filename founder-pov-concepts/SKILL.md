# Founder POV concepts

Short concept bullets a founder or exec can riff on for camera or LinkedIn. High level, opinionated, grounded in buyer research.

## Inputs

Read `company-context.md` first (repo root, or search the workspace for it) and use the fields that apply to this skill. If the file or a line is missing, ask once, then offer to save the answer back to it. Never fill a gap with an assumption about the company.

- Company and speaker: `<fill in>`
- Topics (one concept each): `<fill in>`
- Buyer research and quote library: `<fill in>`
- The speaker's voice: words they use, words they avoid, known positions, past posts or talks: `<fill in>`

If the research or the speaker's voice is missing, ask before drafting.

## Format per concept

- **What I'm seeing change:** one line.
- **Point 1:** one line.
- **Point 2:** one line.
- **What I think:** the speaker's take on the change, in their voice.
- **What it means for [the buyer's field]:** where that role or team goes next.
- **Quote or stat they could use:** one verbatim quote or sourced stat from the library, attributed by role and sector only.

Keep every line to one sentence. No product pitch unless asked; the take is the point.

## Picking the quote or stat

- Search the library by topic and keep quotes short and spoken-sounding.
- Prefer a number the viewer can picture, ideally with its scale (a count against company size, or a time against a simple task).
- Check each quote's usage label. Internal-only quotes get flagged as needing sign-off before going on camera.
- Copy quotes character for character from the source, never from notes.

## Writing rules

Plain language, the speaker's own words where possible. No contrast constructions ("not X, it's Y"), no em dashes, no uplift lines, no rule-of-three reflex.
