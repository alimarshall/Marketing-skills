# Competitor Ad Analysis

Extract why a reference communicates clearly, then apply that principle to the current company's buyer tension, brand and product. A competitor's creative is neither evidence about our product nor evidence of ad performance.

## Inspect the references

Read each supplied ad, not just its caption. Separate the creative from the social platform frame, comments, counters and CTA chrome. Record placement, crop, visible copy, focal subject, composition, typography, color, proof, product treatment and narrative. Distinguish separate concepts from duplicate placements or screenshots of the same ad.

If research beyond supplied files is requested, use accessible sources and record links and dates. Do not infer targeting, spend, conversion, longevity or winner status from the existence of an ad, engagement counts or a screenshot.

Identify the reusable mechanism: a buyer-recognizable question, human moment, single bold contrast, clear artifact, before/after, completed process or proof hierarchy. Explain the function of layout choices, not just their appearance. Note weaknesses that matter to the user's brief, such as unreadable UI or vague category cues.

## Translate, do not reskin

Map the mechanism to our verified buyer evidence and offering. Rewrite the underlying story and use the current company's own identity, photography and actual product assets. Avoid recreating distinctive branded artwork, copying slogans or swapping logos into a near-identical design. When a quote is needed to discuss a reference, keep it brief and attributed.

Offer meaningfully different interpretations where useful. For example, translate a spreadsheet-replacement metaphor into the actual repetitive artifact our buyer wants to stop rebuilding; do not assume our product replaces spreadsheets. A competitor's speed claim does not license the same claim for us.

## Deliver a usable brief

A compact comparison may contain reference, buyer tension, communication mechanism, transferable principle, original direction, needed evidence and visual notes. Include enough detail for `buyer-research-to-ads` or `ad-creative-direction` to act without copying the source.

Respect explicit feedback about how close the treatment should feel, while keeping brand and claim accuracy. For artwork, follow the relevant production skill. Preserve reference provenance in review notes; do not include competitor logos in the finished asset unless deliberately requested and appropriate to the message.

Use `ads-workflow` when available for coordination. Skip competitor analysis when the user has already chosen a direction and no reference question remains; it is an optional input, not a compulsory campaign stage.
