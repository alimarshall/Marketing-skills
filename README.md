# marketing-skills

Claude skills for B2B SaaS product marketing. Positioning, paid creative, content planning, and the verification discipline that keeps all three honest.

One shared spine: **never invent a product fact, a customer quote, a stat or a competitor capability.**

## Using these for any company

Use [`company-context.md`](./company-context.md) as the shared starting point: the company, products, capability baseline, evidence sources, output folders and house style. Fill it in once. For a different company, copy the file and fill it in for that company. No skill hard-codes a brand, product, customer or tool, so nothing else changes. If a line is blank, the skill asks once and offers to save the answer back.

### The buyer research loop

`vertical-persona-research` builds the function and industry view and the industry buying-committee maps. `abm-account-multithread` starts from that map for a named account, and writes new persona insight back. `customer-proof-from-usage-reports` turns usage reports into guardrailed stats that both of them pull into their proof sections. `tracking-audit-punch-list` makes sure the campaigns built on all of this can be measured. Each skill has a **Works with** section naming its inputs and hand-offs; any skill named there that isn't installed is done inline.

### The competitive and buyer research loop

`competitive-intel-system` is the hub. `buyer-call-synthesis` and `competitor-buyer-sweep` bring new evidence in from calls, chat and competitor sites. `buyer-research` files buyer quotes into themes and `competitor-dossiers` files competitor intel. `competitive-matrices` turns both into ratings, the communication matrix and the differentiation reference. When your own product ships, `product-capability-refresh` updates the capability baseline and `claim-propagation-sweep` fixes every doc that still says the old thing. `use-case-evidence-research`, `call-research-trend-analysis` and `research-data-charts` cut the same evidence by use case and over time. All of them read the same competitor, document-location and settled-decision section of `company-context.md`, and check quotes with `scripts/verify_quotes.py`.

### Attribution

`crm-channel-attribution-review` shows which channels produce pipeline and wins. `deal-source-verification` checks the deals those numbers rest on. `paid-media-roi-and-cac` puts spend against the result.

## How they fit together

`creative-strategy` reads the avatar library as an input. `ad-avatars` maintains it. Keeping them separate stops a campaign run from quietly rewriting the research it is standing on.

`positioning-brief` decides what to say about a product change. `creative-strategy` turns a settled message into ads. `research-led-content-plan` turns research into a calendar. Running creative off an unsettled position is the most common way a campaign burns a month.

`capability-boundary-map` is what the other five check claims against. `claim-verification` and `copy-register-check` are the last two steps of any workflow producing customer-facing copy. `source-doc-writeback` is what you run when verification finds that a source document, rather than the copy, is the thing that is wrong.

`competitor-buyer-sweep` keeps the outward-facing research current and `product-capability-refresh` keeps the inward-facing product truth current. Everything else reads from those two. `capability-pillar-research` feeds `pillar-landing-pages`, and `creative-testing` closes the loop on what `creative-strategy` and `chatgpt-ads` put live.

`exec-tl-context-file` sets down what an exec believes and how they write. `exec-post-skeletons-from-buyer-research` uses that file and the buyer research to plan their posts, one skeleton per pain, for the exec to keep, edit or cut.

A few skills hand off to general marketing skills that are not in this repo (`paid-ads`, `page-cro`, `ad-creative`, `ab-test-setup`, `analytics-tracking`, `gong-transcripts`). They work without them; the hand-off just names where that job goes if you have one installed.

## Ads Workflow: research to reviewed creative

Start with [`ads-workflow`](./ads-workflow/SKILL.md) when the job spans several stages. It coordinates the relevant skills and carries context between them. The existing research, avatar and strategy skills keep their specialist responsibilities. A copy edit can start with the current brief; a visual revision can start with the accepted concept. Neither needs a fresh research project.

### Who owns what

| Layer | Skills | What carries forward |
|---|---|---|
| Company and product truth | [`brand-product-context`](./brand-product-context), [`capability-boundary-map`](./capability-boundary-map), [`positioning-brief`](./positioning-brief) | Current brand guidance, verified capabilities and limits, settled positioning |
| Standing buyer evidence | [`buyer-call-synthesis`](./buyer-call-synthesis), [`buyer-research`](./buyer-research), [`use-case-evidence-research`](./use-case-evidence-research), [`vertical-persona-research`](./vertical-persona-research) | Source-linked quotes, pains, aspirations, objections, use-case and industry context |
| Ad audience profiles | [`ad-avatars`](./ad-avatars) | Evidenced avatar IDs, working situations, committee seats, triggers, awareness states, language, consent and framings to avoid |
| Campaign decisions | [`creative-strategy`](./creative-strategy) | Selected avatars and angles, differentiation, awareness deployment, budget-sized concept set, strategy and production briefs |
| Research-led copy | [`buyer-research-to-ads`](./buyer-research-to-ads) | Traceable angles and specific headlines, supporting lines and captions within the campaign brief |
| Proof and references | [`customer-proof-to-ads`](./customer-proof-to-ads), [`competitor-ad-analysis`](./competitor-ad-analysis) | Scoped proof routes and original treatments informed by references |
| Creative production | [`ad-creative-direction`](./ad-creative-direction), [`product-ui-storytelling`](./product-ui-storytelling) | Compositions, supported product sequences and requested assets |
| Verification, review and learning | [`claim-verification`](./claim-verification), [`copy-register-check`](./copy-register-check), [`ad-library-review`](./ad-library-review), [`creative-testing`](./creative-testing) | Checked copy and claims, current review versions, then measured learning when a test has run |

### A full campaign path

1. **Reuse the shared context.** Read [`company-context.md`](./company-context.md) and the existing capability baseline, brand rules and approved assets. Brand & Product Context can fill or refresh the creative brief using those sources. Keep one source of truth; do not create a competing capability catalogue.
2. **Close relevant research gaps.** The research skills maintain the evidence. Use-case and vertical research supply the relevant cuts. Pass source IDs, exact excerpts, context and usage restrictions forward. Buyer Research to Ads interprets that evidence for ads; it does not silently rewrite the VOC library.
3. **Select or refresh avatars.** Ad Avatars owns the standing library. Creative Strategy and Ads Workflow consume the relevant entries. Route new evidence or a genuinely new audience situation back through the avatar build/refresh process, preserving its history and consent tags.
4. **Make the campaign choices once.** Creative Strategy owns the campaign argument, awareness mix, concept selection, testable volume and briefs. Buyer Research to Ads can develop candidate routes and finished wording within that work. Keep the selected angle IDs and record rejected routes so production does not quietly reopen settled decisions.
5. **Add proof or references where useful.** [`customer-proof-from-usage-reports`](./customer-proof-from-usage-reports) extracts and scopes the figures; Customer Proof to Ads turns that verified material into creative. Competitor dossiers and matrices inform positioning; Competitor Ad Analysis studies the ad treatment. Neither a reference ad nor an internal quote proves a product claim or ad performance.
6. **Build the chosen experience.** Ad Creative Direction translates the brief into a composition. Product UI Storytelling shapes the supported request, action, decision and result when UI helps. Carry the actual interface sources and capability boundaries into production. On Creative Strategy jobs, follow its rule against mocked product screenshots and use approved actual product captures. Keep text as editable layers and check mobile reading size.
7. **Verify and deliver a coherent set.** Run Claim Verification and Copy Register Check against the finished copy and current evidence. Ad Library & Review keeps the concept IDs, captions, source notes, previews, exports and feedback aligned. Preserve accepted work and remove rejected components from every active use.
8. **Close the learning loop.** Creative Testing owns the test design and interpretation after delivery or launch. Feed measured findings into the learning log and route relevant avatar/research changes back to their owners. Producing a concept is not evidence that it works.

### Match the creative to the ad angle

Before choosing an image, state the visual job: **because the ad is about this buyer tension, show this moment or artifact so the viewer understands this change.** The scene, headline and product evidence should tell the same story. Brand colours and an attractive photo alone do not establish that fit.

| Angle | What the creative can make visible |
|---|---|
| Repetitive work or chasing | The recurring request, artifact or handoff and a supported outcome |
| Leadership, ambition or scale | A credible decision-making or team context that matches the larger promise |
| Help at a specific moment | A plausible situation, the actual question and the support or action provided |
| Visibility and confidence | The relevant question, useful report or decision context |
| Customer proof | A verified result and its meaning, without implying an unrelated person is the customer |

Use photography alone when it carries the angle. Add product UI when it explains a meaningful action; do not add bubbles to every photo or use them to rescue an unrelated scene. Check the full asset at mobile size for story, specificity, claim accuracy and first-read hierarchy. A new photo or crop is an execution variant, not automatically a new ad angle.

See [Match the creative to the angle](./ad-creative-direction/references/angle-to-visual.md) for the treatment map and review checks.

### Source, create and use photos

1. **Brief the moment first.** Specify the action, setting, mood, subject position, copy space and delivery crops. Search or generate against that brief.
2. **Choose the right source.** Use approved company photography, properly licensed stock, a commissioned shoot or generation where the brief permits it. Track the source and planned advertising usage; image search is not a licence.
3. **Make generated scenes plausible.** Describe an ordinary, specific moment with natural light and restrained expressions. Reserve space for copy. Create the scene separately from typography, logos and operational UI, then composite those from the correct assets.
4. **Integrate deliberately.** Protect faces and meaningful gestures. Keep UI readable, use transparency carefully and preserve editable layers where possible. Inspect each crop rather than assuming one layout works everywhere.
5. **Review and maintain.** Check anatomy, props, reflections, lighting and scene logic as well as legibility. Do not imply a stock or generated person is a customer. Record asset IDs, provenance and accepted/rejected uses so revisions reach every active ad.

See [Photos: source, create and use](./ad-creative-direction/references/photos.md) for sourcing decisions, a reusable photo prompt, overlays and quality checks.

### Shared handoffs and boundaries

Carry one compact record per concept: concept ID, avatar ID, use case, awareness state, source IDs, buyer tension, angle, product job, proof and usage status, copy, visual direction, placement, version and review status. Preserve `SAFE`, `NEEDS VERIFY` and `INTERNAL ONLY` tags in internal records. Keep review notes outside customer-facing artwork unless a qualification is required for accuracy.

The new ad skills are unbranded and can support software, goods or services. In this repository, use the existing company context, source restrictions, house style and campaign rules. Their more general defaults do not relax those requirements. For standalone work, a missing companion skill can be handled inline; use only the stages the assignment needs. These handoffs describe responsibilities, not automatic background execution or permission to publish ads, change spend or contact customers.

### Example requests

- **Full campaign:** "Use Ads Workflow with our buyer research and existing avatar library. Have Creative Strategy select a testable concept set, then develop the copy, visuals and review gallery."
- **Use-case copy:** "Use Buyer Research to Ads for the selected avatar and use cases. Keep the current positioning and approved campaign angles."
- **Proof-led creative:** "Use our verified customer proof file to develop stat-led routes, then brief the visuals and add the chosen versions to the review library."
- **Visual revision:** "Keep the approved angle and photo. Fix the product sequence and mobile legibility, then update every affected active export."

See [Project handoffs](./ads-workflow/references/project-handoffs.md) for the reusable records and shorter paths.

## Setup: two edits, once

These skills invent nothing, which means they need your documents.

1. **Fill in [`creative-strategy/references/inputs.md`](./creative-strategy/references/inputs.md)** with your own paths: capability baseline, claim verification tracker, buyer research, messaging matrix, brand voice and writing rules, competitor dossiers. That file is the contract for the whole repo, and it also tells you what a skill does when a document is missing. `positioning-brief` reads the same list.
2. **Fill in the constraint tables** in [`angle-library.md`](./creative-strategy/references/angle-library.md) and [`hooks-and-mechanics.md`](./creative-strategy/references/hooks-and-mechanics.md) against your own brand rules. Both ship as templates listing the angles and hook tactics that are *commonly* constrained in B2B, with the category of constraint that usually blocks each. Filling them in is the highest-value setup step in the whole thing, because it converts brand rules into creative decisions rather than leaving them as a review gate someone fails at the end.

Several newer skills also carry their own short **Inputs** section with `<fill in>` placeholders, and `employee-linkedin-posts` ships `references/company-context.md` as a template to fill in. Record paths in `inputs.md` where you can, so there is one list.

`copy-register-check` also needs your banned-construction list. If one does not exist, build it from the last three rounds of editorial feedback. The rules people actually enforce are the ones they keep correcting.

Nothing else needs changing.

## What the method insists on

**Never invent a product fact, a customer quote, a stat or a competitor capability.** Every claim in a brief is traceable or flagged. Quotes are reproduced word for word from the source file, never from working notes, because the tidied version always reads better and that is exactly the problem.

**Consent travels with the quote.** Anything from a sales call, win/loss or a named customer is internal-use-only until sign-off exists. It can shape an angle and sit in a brief under that banner. It cannot be the ad.

**Three things get confused and must stay separate.** Confirmed to work, safe to say publicly, and confirmed by a customer. A capability can be live and un-announceable. An engineer confirming it works is not a case study.

**Angle before execution, and test angles against each other.** A strong angle survives a weak hook. Running five hooks off one angle and calling it creative testing tells you about wording and nothing about the argument.

**Unclaimed is not defensible.** White space found only in a teardown is a guess. Cross it against your own buyer research, then apply the swap test: could a competitor say this truthfully too?

**A verification claim is worth nothing without the output.** The sweep counts when it is run mechanically over the finished text, after the last edit, and reports what it found including the zeroes. This is the single most repeated failure across every skill here.

**Price the set before you write it.** Budget divided by variants divided by weeks, against what the decision actually needs. A twelve-concept set handed to a budget that can read three is a wasted week.

## Two things it is deliberately honest about

**No ad library exposes performance for ordinary commercial ads.** Not spend, not click-through, not conversions. What a teardown gives you is longevity and variant repetition, which are hypothesis signals. `competitor-teardown.md` also documents, verified against a live ad, exactly how much the EU transparency layer does and does not disclose, because most secondary write-ups overstate it.

**There is no platform or industry creative-diversity score.** The six-dimension check in `creative-matrix.md` is defined in this repo and labelled as such, rather than dressed up as a standard.

## Volume: pick the right branch

The high-volume performance-creative canon and low-volume B2B reality are two disciplines sharing a job title, and averaging them produces guidance that fits neither. `creative-matrix.md` section 4 branches: sharply targeted B2B audiences at high CPMs get two ads per campaign, two full weeks minimum, and a call at 80 to 85 per cent confidence, because waiting for 95 at that volume means never deciding. The weekly conveyor and the concept-count tables belong to the other branch.


## Evidence-to-pages workflow

Four skills that run in order, from raw product docs and sales calls to verified evidence, positioning pillars and landing-page briefs, then kept current when the product changes. Each has a "Works with" section and the same eight shared rules (evidence before copy, shipped means shipped, verbatim buyer quotes checked by script, SAFE / NEEDS VERIFY / INTERNAL ONLY tags, thin evidence said plainly, finished docs not change logs, an external-safe mode, plain voice).

| Step | Skill | Takes in | Produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`use-case-evidence-research`](./use-case-evidence-research) | Product docs, call transcripts, VoC library | One verified evidence doc per use case or feature (internal or external-safe) |
| 2 | [`capability-pillar-research`](./capability-pillar-research) | Step 1 docs and the product's capability catalogue | One doc per verb-based pillar, with an evidence-strength rating |
| 3 | [`pillar-landing-pages`](./pillar-landing-pages) | Positioning brief and steps 1-2 | One landing-page brief per pillar, with its own keywords and copy |
| 4 | [`claim-propagation-sweep`](./claim-propagation-sweep) | A product change and everything from steps 1-3 | Fixed docs, a flag list, and live assets to change by hand |

**Quote check.** [`use-case-evidence-research/verify_quotes.py`](./use-case-evidence-research/verify_quotes.py) checks every quoted bullet in a doc against your raw sources and exits non-zero on anything not found:

```
python3 -I use-case-evidence-research/verify_quotes.py path/to/doc.md path/to/transcripts/ path/to/voc.csv
```
