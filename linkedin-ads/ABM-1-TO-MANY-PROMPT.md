# Prompt: Build 1:many LinkedIn ABM campaigns

Copy the prompt below into a new chat and replace the bracketed inputs. Keep company evidence, account lists and campaign records in your private workspace. This is a scalable named-account motion; a large audience alone does not make a campaign ABM.

```text
Use the linkedin-ads skill and its targeting, lists, funnels, build-from-scratch and measurement guidance in https://github.com/alimarshall/Marketing-skills.

Build a 1:many LinkedIn ABM program for [our company website], using [approved target-account universe or criteria to research it], in [markets], within [budget, currency and period]. Use [private company context, CRM evidence and approved assets, or identify what is missing].

Validate our ICP against available customer and commercial evidence. Build or clean a defensible named-account universe with fit criteria, disqualifiers, canonical company identities, source evidence and version/refresh ownership. Recommend useful segments only when they change messaging, spend control or measurement. Avoid both a generic broad audience labelled ABM and an unnecessarily fragmented campaign structure.

Map relevant buying roles and create scalable messages and approved proof for their shared problems. Design an appropriate mix of education/TLA/video, content acquisition, relevant warm nurture and evaluation offers. Use ebooks or webinars where the offer and follow-up are ready; do not force every account through every stage.

Prepare company-list upload files where authorized source data is available; otherwise specify exact source requirements and outstanding work. Use the current LinkedIn template and verify matching, source quality and final eligible members after geography, role filters and exclusions. Keep company lists, contact lists and behavioral audiences distinct.

Write explicit targeting expressions and an inclusion/exclusion matrix. Keep the approved company universe a real constraint for strict ABM, including the relevant retargeting paths. Decide overlap and precedence with 1:1 accounts and 1:few clusters; suppress customers, pipeline and offer submitters according to each motion. Treat predictive or expanded prospecting as a separately labelled test, not delivery restricted to the named-account universe.

Produce the audience register, campaign/ad-set hierarchy, list files or source gaps, creative briefs/drafts, offer and CRM handoffs, budget rationale, measurement setup and reviewable campaign drafts where tools permit. Plan for warm-pool readiness, source enrollment for new videos/TLAs, combined frequency, list refresh and launch checks.

Measure target-account coverage, relevant role reach, qualified responses, meetings and mature opportunity outcomes. Define how sales receives useful signals and how accounts qualify for a more personal 1:few or 1:1 play based on fit, value, timing and sales capacity. Do not treat engagement alone as buying intent or promise automatic CRM/attendance sync without verification.

Verify current platform mechanics, separate evidence from hypotheses, keep company data private and state missing access or decisions. Prepare everything for review before paid launch; make live changes only within my explicit authorization.
```
