# Prompt: Build 1:few LinkedIn ABM campaigns

Copy the prompt below into a new chat and replace the bracketed inputs. Keep company evidence, account lists and campaign records in your private workspace. This is a cluster-level personalization motion; tier sizes depend on your company and sales capacity.

```text
Use the linkedin-ads guidance, especially targeting-and-abm and the build-from-scratch blueprint, in https://github.com/alimarshall/Marketing-skills. Use abm-account-multithread where deeper account or committee research helps.

Build 1:few LinkedIn ABM campaigns for [our company website] using [approved account list, proposed clusters or account-selection criteria], in [markets], within [budget, currency and period]. Use [private company context, CRM evidence and approved assets, or identify what is missing].

Research the accounts and group them into defensible clusters by shared problem, use case, industry, verified technology or buying trigger. Explain why each cluster deserves a shared campaign, its fit criteria and disqualifiers. Recommend only as many clusters as our budget and sales coverage can support; do not choose an arbitrary account count or treat an unverified technology label as fact.

For each cluster, map the relevant buying committee and develop a distinct narrative, approved proof, offer, creative briefs and appropriate landing-page or form experience. Personalize around the cluster's shared situation rather than inserting company names into generic copy. Use account-specific research where a priority member needs it.

Prepare the master-list rules, cleaned company lists where authorized data is available, source/version records and an explicit targeting expression: geography AND approved cluster companies AND relevant roles MINUS appropriate exclusions. Check current upload requirements, matching and final eligible audience size. Show how overlap with 1:1 accounts, other clusters, customers, pipeline and offer submitters will be handled. Do not pad lists or broaden outside the cluster without an explicit recommendation.

Design the smallest useful cold, warm and evaluation or pipeline-support paths for each cluster. Use TLA/video, proof, guides or webinars where they fit. Keep strict cluster targeting on the relevant warm paths; record source audience IDs, thresholds/windows, suppression, CRM routing and sales owners. Separate expanded prospecting from strict ABM.

Produce the campaign/ad-set plan, targeting and exclusion matrix, list files or missing source requirements, creative drafts/briefs, budget rationale, measurement, launch checks and staged reviewable drafts where tools permit. Evaluate account coverage, qualified responses, meetings and account progression; keep sourced and influenced pipeline distinct. Define refresh, sales handoff and criteria for moving an account into a 1:1 play.

Verify current platform mechanics, distinguish evidence from hypotheses, and keep outputs company-neutral in the public repo and company-specific in our private workspace. Prepare everything for review before paid launch; make live changes only within my explicit authorization.
```
