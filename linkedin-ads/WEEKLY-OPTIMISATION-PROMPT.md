# Weekly LinkedIn optimisation prompt

Copy the entire prompt below into a new or ongoing chat. Fill in known inputs; leave the rest as "unknown". Reuse it when you want a review. Saving this prompt does not schedule reviews or authorize live account changes. Keep completed reviews and company evidence in the company's private workspace.

```text
Use https://github.com/alimarshall/Marketing-skills to run this company's weekly LinkedIn ads performance and optimisation review.

Read linkedin-ads/SKILL.md, references/measurement-and-optimization.md and references/budget-and-testing.md. Use references/targeting-and-abm.md, references/lists-and-audiences.md, references/funnels-and-offers.md and references/source-and-handoff-qa.md for the relevant findings. Use references/launch-and-change.md to prepare any recommended change for review. Do not load unrelated references or replace the company's existing private trackers.

COMPANY AND REVIEW INPUTS

- Company website and priority product/use case: [URL/details or unknown]
- LinkedIn account: [URL/account or unknown]
- Private company context, ICP and campaign plan: [locations or unknown]
- Private prior review, learning log and change log: [locations or unknown]
- Review ending date and account timezone: [date/timezone or use latest available complete account day]
- Approved media ceiling, currency and period: [details or unknown]
- Primary commercial goal and qualification criteria: [details or unknown]
- Approved efficiency targets, test ceilings and scaling policy: [details or unknown]
- CRM, lead-quality, sales-feedback and event sources: [locations or unknown]
- Upcoming offers, webinars, deadlines or business changes: [details or unknown]
- Private output location: [location or existing company workspace]

Use supplied and connected company sources before asking me for information. Continue independent analysis when a field or source is missing. Distinguish unavailable data from zero, observed facts from hypotheses, and a recommendation from a saved or live change. Never invent performance, account membership, CRM outcomes, authorization or access.

This is a read-only review and recommendation request. Do not pause, launch, change budgets/bids/targeting, upload or replace lists, modify CRM rules, request permissions, send messages or submit leads from this prompt alone. Prepare exact proposed changes and any safe local drafts for review. If separate current instructions authorize execution, apply only that defined scope and verify the saved result; report execution separately.

1. ESTABLISH A COMPARABLE REVIEW

Resolve the actual account, currency, timezone, product, approved plan and primary success action. Read the previous review and change log: what was proposed, actually implemented, rolled back or deliberately left unchanged? Check whether those changes had enough time to be evaluated. Do not reissue an unchanged recommendation as a new finding or change a healthy campaign merely because another week has passed.

By default compare the latest seven complete account days with the preceding seven, using explicit dates and excluding today from the main comparison. Add a 28-day trend and month-to-date spend/remaining budget when available. Separately flag urgent intraday problems. Match weekday mix, scheduled days, metric definitions and attribution basis; explain launch timing, seasonality, event timing and material configuration changes that make comparisons imperfect.

Follow lead/acquisition cohorts through their actual qualification and sales lag. Compare similarly aged cohorts or state when an outcome comparison is immature. Do not divide this week's spend by all historical opportunities or assume missing recent CRM outcomes mean failure. Keep sourced, influenced and incremental pipeline distinct.

2. COLLECT EVIDENCE AND CHECK RELIABILITY

Discover a sufficient LinkedIn connector, authorized browser session or supplied exports. Another platform's Ads Manager is not a LinkedIn connector. The repository supplies instructions, not credentials or live performance. If access is incomplete, complete the accessible review, identify the missing fields/source and qualify dependent conclusions.

Cover active entities, recent spenders, recently changed/paused entities and strategically relevant tests. Include their parent status, schedules, remaining caps, objective, optimized action, bid mode, format, offer and saved targeting. Inspect actual settings for recommended changes rather than deriving them from names. State filters, pagination, reporting cutoff, coverage and any sampling; do not claim an exhaustive creative or CRM audit from aggregate rows.

Collect matching spend, impressions, reach/frequency where available, objective-appropriate clicks/CTR, CPC/CPM, video attention, form opens/submissions, valid or work-email leads, registrations/attendance, qualified leads, held qualified meetings, opportunities and pipeline where supported. Show counts and spend behind rates. Calculate aggregate costs from matching totals, not averages of row CPC/CPL. Use blanks for unavailable or zero-denominator rates. Do not compare engagement clicks, website visits, message actions and qualified conversions as equivalent results.

Check the primary conversion definition, attribution windows/model, reporting lag, event status, deduplication and CRM joins. A page visit is not a submitted demo; a selected or inactive conversion rule alone does not prove a working or broken handoff. If primary measurement is unreliable, prioritize diagnosis and mark performance-dependent scale/pause conclusions provisional. Do not claim that an untested flow was tested.

3. REVIEW EACH PART OF THE SYSTEM

- Delivery and budgets: check account/review/billing state, parent holds and dates, ad approval, permissions, budget headroom, pacing, eligibility, bids and saturation before recommending more spend. Reconcile the remaining approved ceiling, actual flight dates and total simultaneous spend. Distinguish shared campaign allocation from separately funded ad sets.
- Creative and offers: compare relevant reach, attention, response and lead quality within comparable audiences and formats. Investigate creative fatigue against audience pressure and delivery changes rather than assuming any CTR drop means fatigue. Identify the exact creative/post, message, proof, CTA or offer to refresh. Check source-post reuse and permissions before proposing an edit that could affect multiple ad sets. Inspect affected destinations, card-level URLs, tracking and form/asset delivery where evidence warrants it.
- Targeting and ABM: compare approved ICP/accounts/markets with saved geography/residency, employer pages or list IDs, titles or function/seniority, size bands, behavior constraints, AND/OR logic, exclusions, expansion and placements. Inspect available delivered company/role demographics and CRM lead quality; respect aggregate-reporting limits. Flag extra employers/geographies, over-narrowing, irrelevant delivery and accidental broadening. Separate a strict account constraint from a predictive/expansion signal. For 1:1, 1:few and 1:many, assess actual account/cluster committee coverage, account progression, overlap and sales feedback; do not judge 1:1 air cover by raw lead CPL alone.
- Company and contact lists: review source owner/query/version, freshness relative to the source's cadence, processing/sync failures, matching exceptions, current matched size, final eligible size and all affected inclusion/exclusion dependencies. Preserve company versus contact object type and canonical parent/subsidiary identity. High match rate and Ready status are not proof of ICP quality. Source rows, matched members and final eligible members are different counts. Identify lifecycle, employment and account changes that actually justify a refresh. If original membership/query is inaccessible, specify the needed source work without pretending it was verified.
- Warm audiences and exclusions: verify actual selected Page, TLA, video, document, form and website sources; lookback/threshold; new-content enrollment; replenishment; expiry; and repeated exposure across overlapping cells. Compare customer, pipeline, employee, same-offer converter and registrant suppression with each motion's intended policy. Do not suppress valid customer expansion blindly. Do not infer named-person exposure or exact audience overlap from aggregate reporting that does not provide it.
- Ebooks, webinars and sales handoffs: distinguish capture from qualification; registration from attendance/no-show; booking from a held meeting. Check asset access, event cutoff/timezone, registration suppression, stale invitation copy, follow-up owner and response quality. Verify the actual event/CRM source before recommending attendance-based lists. Do not invent an integration or send a test message/production lead as a side effect of the review.

4. RECOMMEND WHAT TO DO

Assign a primary decision at the appropriate ad, ad-set, campaign, audience/list or offer level:

- PAUSE: recommend stopping a specific entity when evidence supports an urgent failure, incorrect targeting, closed event, exhausted test ceiling or unacceptable matured quality/economics. Check whether pausing the parent would also stop healthy work. Separate immediate technical/eligibility issues from inconclusive performance.
- SCALE: recommend more allocation only when measurement, matured quality/economics, eligible supply and sales capacity support it. Show exact current/proposed daily or flight budget, absolute and percentage change, parent-cap implications, total account effect, funding source and observation period. Follow the company's agreed policy; the budget guide's example percentages are adjustable heuristics, not automatic rules or permission to exceed the ceiling.
- REFRESH: recommend an exact creative/offer/source-post change with the problem it addresses, evidence, required approved proof/asset/permission and a test hypothesis. Preserve a useful control; do not replace every ad at once without a reason.
- CHANGE TARGETING: show the exact before/after audience expression and facets/IDs, expected reach/quality tradeoff, exclusion and overlap effects, and whether saved settings can be edited or need a new test. Broader reach is not automatically better. Preserve strict named-account intent unless a separately approved strategy changes it.
- UPDATE LISTS: name the audience and authoritative source/version, the addition/removal/correction rule, why now, affected campaigns and exclusions, owner, matching/processing checks and rollback. Prepare a validated local file or precise source specification where authorized data is available; do not upload, replace membership or create fictional contacts.
- INVESTIGATE: identify the smallest diagnostic, missing source or business decision that could change the recommendation; give an owner and the consequence of waiting. Do not confuse diagnosis with a completed fix.
- HOLD: explain why the setup should remain stable, what evidence is still maturing and the next review/stop condition. Insufficient evidence is a valid reason to hold a funded test within its ceiling. No change is a valid weekly outcome.

Do not use universal CTR/CPL/frequency cutoffs, copy another company's benchmarks or declare a winner from a tiny immature sample. Use the current company's approved economics, funded test plan and comparable evidence. Recommend reallocating or staging cells when budget is fragmented; do not count the same ABM allowance twice in funnel totals. Make the smallest interpretable change and note material confounders.

5. DELIVER A DECISION-READY PRIVATE REVIEW

Start with the main conclusion and the few highest-impact priorities, including what is healthy and should stay unchanged. Explain each in plain language and state confidence and its limits.

Provide:

A. A concise scorecard by motion/campaign: current/prior spend and relevant outcomes, absolute and percentage changes where meaningful, matured quality results, primary decision and evidence links. Group unchanged low-risk entities when useful, but list which IDs the group covers. Identify unreviewed scope explicitly.

B. An action table: priority; PAUSE/SCALE/REFRESH/CHANGE TARGETING/UPDATE LISTS/INVESTIGATE/HOLD; exact entity names/IDs; evidence with period, counts and spend; cause versus hypothesis; confidence; exact before/after proposal; expected benefit/tradeoff; budget and shared-dependency effect; owner; verification/rollback; next review condition; and execution/authorization state. Do not imply a recommendation has already been applied. Put missing inputs alongside the affected action rather than burying them.

C. Targeting/list changes: proposed Boolean differences and audience/source IDs, inclusion versus exclusion use, source/query/version requirements, dependency/overlap effects, refresh owner, matching/eligibility checks and any prepared local files. Keep private contacts and source rows out of the report when a protected pointer is sufficient.

D. A seven-day plan: prioritized diagnostics, creative briefs/tests, source refreshes, event actions and sales/RevOps handoffs, with owners, review dates and budget guardrails. Show what to defer and why. Preserve ongoing experiments unless evidence warrants intervention.

E. Continuity: compare prior recommendations with observed implementation and results; update the existing private learning, audience and change records as appropriate. Record the next review window, open dependencies and what evidence would reverse a decision. A review-only entry must not claim a live setting changed.

Save the review and any local drafts in the current company's private workspace. Keep this public repository unbranded and free of account details, membership, contacts and unpublished performance. Verify current official platform guidance when a proposed change depends on a changeable mechanic. Finish the accessible analysis and concrete proposals before asking for an unresolved decision or authorization.
```
