# Funnels and offers

## Separate strategy from the platform objective

Funnel stage describes the buyer's readiness and the job of the message. Objective describes the action LinkedIn optimizes delivery toward. Format describes how the ad appears. Audience describes who is eligible. These are four independent decisions: an Engagement campaign can do awareness work; an ebook in a folder called Consideration can still target cold people.

| Motion | Audience | Message and offer | Possible objective/format | Meaningful evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demand creation | Qualified cold people or selected accounts | Useful problem framing, executive POV, category education | Awareness, Engagement or Video views with eligible formats | Qualified reach, attention and warm-pool growth |
| Demand capture/content acquisition | Cold or warm ICP, deliberately chosen | Relevant ebook, guide, benchmark or webinar | Lead generation with form; website route when appropriate | Valid ICP contacts, asset access and downstream response |
| Evaluation nurture | Relevant engaged people | Use case, customer proof, practical walkthrough, objection response | Engagement, Website visits, suitable video/document/image | Qualified visits, return interest, meetings |
| Conversion | High-intent prospects or eligible cold direct-response test | Demo, evaluation, consultation or trial | Website conversions or Lead generation | Held qualified meetings, opportunities and economics |
| Pipeline support | Open-opportunity account committees | Technical proof, peer validation, business case, event | Suitable awareness/engagement/visit format | Committee coverage, stage progression and sales feedback |
| Customer expansion | Relevant customer accounts | New use case, product education and adoption | Separate audience, offer and measurement | Adoption, qualified expansion and retention outcomes |

These are candidate choices, not a fixed template. Check current objective/format eligibility. Some buyers skip content offers or arrive ready for a demo; do not force every prospect through an ebook and webinar sequence. Several warm layers may need pooling to reach a usable audience.

## Thought Leader Ads, including video

TLA sponsors an eligible member's existing public post under their identity. The author's followers are not automatically the paid audience. Choose paid targeting independently. Verify Page permissions, ad-account access, source post eligibility, author sponsorship permission and the public post URL. Drafting a post is different from publishing it or requesting permission from its author.

Current official eligibility checked 18 November 2026: text/single-image TLAs support Brand awareness and Engagement; video TLAs also support Video views; other supported post types have their own restrictions. Native CTA buttons are unavailable for standard single-image, video and document TLAs; URLs can appear in the intro text, and Event TLAs differ. Recheck before building. [TLA formats and objectives](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1399568/thought-leader-ads).

Choose the optimization goal to match the job: Video views buys video attention; Engagement buys the defined engagement action. Neither is a substitute for measuring qualified demand. A high engagement CTR may contain social/profile activity, so compare landing-page clicks separately. Inspect the source post's author, message and assets rather than assuming an ad name containing TLA proves the format.

Practical sequence:

1. Use evidence-led POV or a useful explanation for cold ICP or an account cluster. Keep the product's relevance clear.
2. Define eligible video-viewer or ad-engager audiences with explicit source ad sets and lookback. Start with a threshold that balances attention quality and volume; 25% of a long video is different from 25% of a short one.
3. Offer a deeper asset, related proof or webinar to that warm audience when large enough. Maintain necessary ICP fit and appropriate exclusions.
4. Offer evaluation to people showing relevant interest; continue education for others. CRM intent and sales stage can override a simplistic behavior ladder.

Illustrative logical flow:

```text
Cold ICP / named accounts
  → eligible executive video TLA
  → selected-source video viewers at chosen threshold and recency
  → related proof or learning offer
  → qualified evaluation / sales follow-up

Open opportunities
  → separate committee support with proof matched to current objections
```

A new campaign does not automatically join an existing audience source set. After publishing an eligible new TLA, inspect and update the source selections where authorized. If the source type cannot capture the desired behavior, use an eligible alternative or a correctly tracked website destination. Do not claim universal retargeting of organic engagers, profile visitors or all TLA formats.

## Ebook and document programs

Decide what the asset helps the buyer do, which ICP it is for, and why the company has credible evidence. Choose among an ungated document for distribution/education, a gated Lead Gen Form, or a website download flow. A document ad is a format; an ebook is an offer; they are not synonymous.

Before launch, finish the ad, asset, preview/gating arrangement, form, privacy-policy link, thank-you action, delivery location, CRM mapping and nurture owner. Capture only fields needed for qualification/routing; extra friction needs a reason. Use form hidden metadata where supported to carry the asset and campaign IDs. Keep asset download separate from sales acceptance.

Define useful cohorts: document engagers; form openers who did not submit; submitters for this asset; qualified submitters; existing opportunities; customers. Exclude submitters from the same acquisition offer and show them an appropriate next asset. Do not automatically mark every ebook lead as sales-ready or send an unsolicited sales sequence without the appropriate company authorization and permitted use.

Measure valid leads, ICP-fit share, engagement with delivered content, qualified meetings and opportunity creation by cohort. Compare qualified cost, not only CPL. Cheap downloads with poor qualification can be worse than fewer relevant contacts. [Lead Gen Form capabilities](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a423447), [CRM integration and testing](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a425316/integrate-lead-gen-forms-with-your-marketing-automation-crm-system).

## Webinar programs and the different lists

Keep these objects distinct:

| List | Source | Correct use |
|---|---|---|
| Target companies to invite | Approved company universe | Relevant employees at those accounts |
| Named invite contacts | Authorized CRM/event list | Matchable contacts, subject to size and permitted use |
| Warm people to invite | Relevant behavior audiences | Prospects familiar with the subject/company |
| Registrants | Registration system or LinkedIn form/event | Suppress acquisition invitations; deliver permitted reminders |
| Attendees | Actual attendance data | Recording, relevant deeper content, questions and sales handoff |
| No-shows | Registrants minus actual attendees | Recording and an easier way to engage |
| Engaged non-registrants | Eligible event/ad behavior | A clear benefit or alternate time/recording |
| Open-opportunity accounts | CRM company lifecycle | Sales-coordinated invitations and evaluation support |

An externally hosted webinar, a LinkedIn Event, a Lead Gen Form and an Event Ad can create different records. Agree which system is the registration truth and which is attendance truth. A button click to an event page is not a completed registration. Native Event audiences now offer attendance-related options for eligible LinkedIn Events; inspect availability. [Event audiences](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a421211/).

Plan three phases:

- **Before:** choose topic/audience, host, timezone, real date, registration destination, reminder owner and consent wording; connect form/event/CRM; build registrant suppression. Use separately funded cold, warm and pipeline invitation cells only where scale supports them.
- **During:** stop expired acquisition creative at the event cutoff; use the confirmed attendee source and record questions/engagement appropriately. Ads are not a dependable substitute for a registered attendee reminder system.
- **After:** reconcile registration and attendance; update cohorts, suppress obsolete invitations, deliver recording/answers and coordinate relevant sales follow-up. Use the recording for a new on-demand offer with its own naming and dates.

Never promise automatic attendance sync unless verified. Small attendee/no-show lists may need CRM follow-up or pooling across truly related events rather than standalone ads. Combining lists must preserve relevance and not mislabel non-attendees as attendees.

## Messaging, Spotlight and mixed formats

Treat messaging and right-rail formats as separate tests with appropriate creative, sender rights, inventory and measurements. Message sends/opens and website visits have different denominators; do not rank them together using generic CTR or CPM. Inspect the specific metric definitions. Verify regional eligibility: current EEA/Swiss Sponsored Messaging is available to opted-in members, so a blanket claim that it is banned there is outdated. [Regional guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a544586).

Use the smallest useful format mix first. The presence of message, event and Spotlight campaigns in another account is evidence of testing, not a reason to reproduce all formats at a new company.
