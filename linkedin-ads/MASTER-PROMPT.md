# Master prompt: Build a new company's LinkedIn ads program

Copy the entire prompt below into a new chat. Fill in what you know and leave the rest marked "unknown". Keep completed company inputs and research in that company's private workspace.

```text
Use https://github.com/alimarshall/Marketing-skills as your operating playbook to research this company and build its LinkedIn advertising program from scratch.

Read `linkedin-ads/SKILL.md`, its build-from-scratch blueprint, and the relevant targeting, lists, funnels, launch and measurement references. Use other skills in the repository where they materially help with company context, buyer research, creative or attribution.

**Company and inputs**

- Company website: [URL]
- Products/use cases to prioritize: [details or unknown]
- Private company context and evidence: [locations or unknown]
- LinkedIn Campaign Manager account: [URL or not created]
- Markets/geographies: [details or recommend]
- Media budget, currency and period: [details or unknown]
- Commercial goal: [qualified meetings, pipeline, trials, etc.]
- CRM and available customer/sales evidence: [details or unknown]
- Existing content, customer proof, ebooks, webinars and executive posts: [locations or unknown]
- Launch timing and practical constraints: [details or unknown]

**Your responsibility**

Act as our LinkedIn advertising strategist and operator. Research the business, make evidence-backed recommendations, and turn them into concrete targeting, list specifications, funnel designs and reviewable campaign builds. Explain decisions in plain language so I can understand and maintain the system.

Use connected sources and existing company materials before asking me for information. Ask concise questions only when a missing decision materially affects the work. Continue independent work while waiting. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses and recommendations; never invent customer evidence, account membership, performance or access.

**1. Research the company and recommend its ICP**

Understand the product, problems solved, differentiation, strongest use cases and credible proof. Where available, examine good-fit wins, retention, expansion, losses, sales calls and CRM outcomes.

Recommend prioritized company segments and buying committees. Explain company-fit criteria, disqualifiers, buying triggers, problem owners, champions, economic buyers and evaluators. Identify what LinkedIn can target directly and what requires an externally researched account list.

When evidence is limited, propose focused test hypotheses and explain how to validate them.

**2. Build the targeting and ABM system**

Recommend native prospecting, company-list targeting, contact targeting and behavioral retargeting where appropriate.

Define practical ABM tiers or clusters based on account value, shared problems, sales coverage and personalization capacity. Avoid arbitrary tier sizes.

Write each proposed audience as explicit logic:

`Geography AND company fit AND person fit AND applicable behavior MINUS exclusions`

Show exact proposed facets, title/function/seniority choices, company-size bands and inclusion/exclusion relationships. Explain overlap between broad prospecting, priority accounts, warm audiences, pipeline and customers. Keep strict ABM separate from expanded prospecting.

**3. Build the list-production process**

Define the master account-list schema, research sources, selection rules, corporate-identity checks, deduplication, tiering, ownership and refresh process.

Identify the company, contact, customer, pipeline and offer-specific lists we need. For webinars, distinguish invite companies, invite contacts, registrants, attendees and no-shows.

Where authorized source data is available, prepare cleaned upload files using the current LinkedIn template. Otherwise, provide the exact source requirements and outstanding work. Verify current upload limits, matching requirements and final audience eligibility. Keep source rows, matched members and final targetable members distinct.

**4. Design the funnels and required assets**

Recommend the smallest useful starting program, then explain how it can grow.

Consider cold education, executive/video Thought Leader Ads, ebooks or guides, proof-led nurture, webinars, evaluation/demo offers and pipeline support. Choose the motions that fit this company.

For every proposed cell, specify its purpose, audience logic, objective, format, message, offer, destination, exclusions, measurement and next step.

Map the dependencies: which videos/posts feed which audiences; what happens after a download or registration; how submitters are suppressed; and how leads reach CRM and the right follow-up owner. Identify required content and permissions. Prepare creative briefs or drafts using approved evidence.

**5. Produce a staged implementation and launch plan**

Provide the proposed campaign/ad-set hierarchy, audience register, exclusion matrix, asset requirements, budget rationale, schedules, measurement setup and launch checks.

Stage deployment around readiness, audience supply and budget. Explain what to launch first, what to prepare but defer, and what evidence justifies adding another cell. Do not force every buyer through one sequence or divide a small budget across too many campaigns.

Prepare reviewable drafts where tools and access permit. Make live changes only within my explicit authorization; this prompt alone does not authorize paid launch.

**6. Establish ongoing operation**

Define delivery checks, lead-routing tests, list refreshes, new TLA source enrollment, webinar lifecycle updates and a practical review cadence.

Measure qualified commercial outcomes alongside platform metrics. Record changes, evidence, results and rollback options.

Save company-specific outputs in our private workspace. Keep the public repository unbranded and free of account data, contacts and unpublished results.

Work through to concrete deliverables within the available access. Finish with the recommended starting configuration, completed artifacts, unresolved dependencies and the exact next actions needed to launch.
```
