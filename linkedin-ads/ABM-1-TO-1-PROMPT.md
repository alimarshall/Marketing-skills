# Prompt: Build 1:1 LinkedIn ABM campaigns

Copy the prompt below into a new chat and replace the bracketed inputs. Keep completed company details, account research and campaign records in your private workspace.

```text
Read `linkedin-ads/references/budget-and-testing.md` in the repository. Adapt the budget ranges to this company's currency, economics and eligible audiences. Show daily pace, actual-flight spend, 30-day equivalent, testing periods, stop/hold/scale rules and total media across overlapping cells. Specify whether each allowance is per account, cluster or account universe; do not double count it with funnel budgets.

Use the linkedin-ads and abm-account-multithread guidance in https://github.com/alimarshall/Marketing-skills.

Build 1:1 LinkedIn ABM campaigns for [accounts] for [our company website], within [budget and period].

Research each account, map its buying committee, develop account-specific messaging and proof, and prepare targeting, exclusions, creative briefs, campaign drafts, measurement and sales handoffs. Check whether each account has sufficient audience size. Use our private company evidence and identify missing inputs. Prepare everything for review before paid launch.
```

Budget reference: [Starting budgets, testing periods and scaling](references/budget-and-testing.md).

For a complete new-company program, use the [LinkedIn ads master prompt](MASTER-PROMPT.md).
